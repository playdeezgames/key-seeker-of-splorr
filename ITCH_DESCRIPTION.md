**Description**

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/key-seeker-of-splorr/

This is my entry for ASCII Game Jam 2023 (theme: Machine). It is a turn-based, text-based, combat-heavy strategic resource management game with RPG elements.

You pick from menus: click or tap a choice, press its number, or use the arrow keys and Enter. Escape picks "Never Mind".

The world is "procedurally generated", which really just means that I employed a maze generator to make everything in the game. There is a town, a sewer in the town that is bigger than the town, a forest, a graveyard, a seven story tower, a ruins, and even a UFO.

In order to proceed, you will need to gather a key to the next section of the game. You start with no keys, and will have to acquire one. In the end, there is a machine with a lever that you have to pull.

Good luck!

**New: it runs in your browser**

The game now plays right on this page. It is a port of the original VB.NET console game to Odin and WebAssembly. The rules, numbers, shops and mazes are the original's.

- Your game saves itself in this browser after every turn, and comes back as "Continue" when you return. "Save Game..." and "Load Game..." are still on the menu and use the same save.
- There is no Quit (it is a tab now).
- The Windows, Linux and macOS downloads are still here and are the original game.
